## 1. One-line thesis

> **We build verified intelligence:** AI that formalizes claims, machine-checks them, and ships only what the ledger proves — with everything else labeled open or conditional.

The pentagonal / Brockian math program is the **crucible**.

## 2. Why this is a company (not “an AI math hobby”)

Every frontier model produces confident, plausible, **unverifiable** output. Enterprise and scientific partners cannot put that into:

- post-quantum migration evidence,
- quantum entropy / security claims,
- or public scientific challenges (SAIR),

…without a mechanical answer to: *Is this proved, conditional, or open?*

We have built that answer as **infrastructure**:

| Layer | What it is | Why it is hard to copy |
|-------|------------|-------------------------|
| **Formal core** | Lean 4 + Mathlib formalization (Brockian Verified Core) | Deep domain + sustained multi-agent campaign |
| **Honesty firewall** | Registers **derived** from axioms + independent check — never hand-painted “PROVED” | Process product; survives marketing pressure |
| **Multi-prover stack** | Axiom/AXLE (verify) · Harmonic/Aristotle (hard classical) · Codex (Weyl/Gate-1) · Grok (reduce-only frontier) | Heterogeneous orchestration over one registry |
| **Registry SSOT** | `registry/theorems.json` → paper, observatory, partner claims | Single source of truth for all surfaces |
| **Problem pipeline** | Domain cards (Erdős, distillation, physics, CS, quantum) → attack → verify → ledger | Generalizes beyond one corpus |

**Brand sentence (ownable):**  
*We ship what is proven and mark what is not.*
