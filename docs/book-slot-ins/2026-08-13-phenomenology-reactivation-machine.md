## Chapter text (essay adapted; the canonical prose is
docs/essays/the-reactivation-machine.md — merge verbatim from there, with these
book-specific substitutions):

1. Open with the two-suitcase frame instead of cold: suggested lead — "The second
   suitcase contained, among its forty thousand pages, a fragment Husserl never prepared
   for publication. This book has quoted the Crisis and left the fragment folded. It is
   time to unfold it, because the fragment is a specification, and the specification has
   now been met — in halves."
2. Where the essay says "the parent project," say "the mathematical program documented in
   The Sixth Intention" (footnote to that volume).
3. Where the essay says "the pages where the corpus lives," name the instrument the way
   Ch. 11 names ACUTIS: a made place, dated, with its failure record public.
4. Close by returning the inverse bet: "The bet was that a phenomenological architecture
   can be operationalized. ACUTIS was the first collateral. This is the second: Husserl's
   reactivation demand, operationalized as a build step — with the boundary stone still
   standing where it stood, because the machine walks the itinerary and experiences no
   landscape. The wheel without the hub can, it turns out, keep the books. What it keeps
   them for remains the hub's affair."
