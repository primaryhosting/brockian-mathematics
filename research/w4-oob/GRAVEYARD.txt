W4 GRAVEYARD (dead ends + wrongness found, per method law)
===========================================================
G-W4-1. REAL-WINDOW WINGS for negative oob spectral mass -- DEAD (algebra).
    A real symmetric window q = core + wing has wing x mirror-wing
    autocorrelation (w+ * w-)(alpha) > 0 near alpha ~ 2 s_wing in (1, 2T]
    (product of two negative wings), so every real-window family leaks a
    POSITIVE out-of-band speck exactly where the sign must be <= 0, unless
    supp q <= [0, 1/2] (= Montgomery-Taylor, no oob reach).  The phase of a
    COMPLEX profile is the degree of freedom that evades this.

G-W4-2. Free-complex SLSQP search (closed_form.py) -- ABANDONED-AS-METHOD.
    (a) constraint margin made the MT start infeasible (hundreds of
    violated rows -> "inequality constraints incompatible"); (b) with the
    margin at 0 the optimizer exploited constraint-GRID slack: its "LB =
    0.713" point violated rho <= 0 between grid points -- above the LP
    adversary value, i.e. self-refuting.  Kept as the derivation +
    calibration file; the search moved to closed_form2/3.

G-W4-3. Structural modulated family (sum of separate squares, env >= 0 at
    frequency phi) -- CANNOT LEAVE V_A (second order, modal_probe.py).
    sup_{env>=0} Q_phi < 0 for all admissible phi in [1/4, 1/2] (top
    unsigned eigenvalues -5e-5 .. -7e-4).  Structural reason: a
    sum-of-separate-squares kernel has NO cross terms (R, J, G additive
    across components), the joint Hessian at MT is block-diagonal, MT is
    optimal on the band block, and each modulated block is <= 0.  The
    improving kernel MUST couple band and oob spectrum inside ONE square
    -- which is exactly what the LP dual kernels do.  (This also explains
    why closed_form2's honest restarts all returned to V_A: the ABNORMAL
    "improvements" were R -> 0 pole artifacts violating the J+G >= R
    identity forced by GUE feasibility.)

G-W4-4. "J+G < R is impossible" -- BANKED AS VALIDITY FILTER.  GUE

    violating it is a bug/artifact detector, used twice (G-W4-2, G-W4-3).
