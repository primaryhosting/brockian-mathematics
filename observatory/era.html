I · The epoch

A third room between cathedral and casino

Classical mathematics built cathedrals: slow, human, permanent. Mechanized proving built casinos of green lights: fast, silent, sometimes fake.

This core builds a third room. A theorem may only be called proved when it is sorry-free, axiom-clean, and re-verified by an independent engine (AXLE) at a pinned Lean and Mathlib version. Badges are derived from the registry — never hand-painted.
